  them. Archived records are excluded from the normal visual content picker.
- Existing private content is preserved: its independent draft is migrated when
  an approved editor next loads it, rather than reset from the public content.

HOW STAFF EDIT
1. Open /admin and sign in with an approved staff account.
2. Choose Edit any page, then select a page, biography, news story or event.
3. Edit with the formatting tools; expand the editing controls when needed.
4. Use Preview as a parent to review the private draft.
5. Save Draft creates a recoverable full-site checkpoint. Autosave is private.
6. Only Publish changes makes the draft visible to families.
7. For PDFs, open Documents and choose Upload replacement PDF for the document.
   Save Draft, then publish only after school review.
